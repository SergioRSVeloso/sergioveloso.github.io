Research

Research themes

My work connects physics, biology and chemistry, from the assembly of small molecules in soft materials to how drugs are released, and how chemical reactions can be controlled.

Theme 01

Functional nanomaterials

Magnetic ferrite nanoparticles

How the composition and structure of ferrite nanoparticles affect their magnetic properties.

Plasmonic nanomaterials

Gold nanorods and silver nanoprisms to convert near-infrared light into heat and combine that response with soft materials.

Theme 02

Drug delivery and controlled release

Stimuli-responsive gels

Design of peptide and polysaccharide gels that control drug release and respond to chemical and physical stimuli.

Nanogels and nanoparticles

Design nanoscale carriers for drug delivery, from magnetic and light-responsive nanogels to polymeric nanoparticles.

Theme 03

Catalysis

Microcapsules and microparticles

Design of microcapsules and microparticles as bioreactor systems for operation of catalytic cascaded reactions, enantioselective transformations and sensor applications.

Molecularly imprinted hydrogels

Design of molecular imprinted hydrogel materials for enhanced catalytic and enantioselective transformations.
