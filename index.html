About

How small molecules build functional structures

I am interested in understanding how very small molecules assemble into complex structures and how these structures can be designed to perform specific chemical functions.

During my PhD at the University of Minho, I developed gels from ultrashort peptides that could release drugs in response to light or magnetic fields. I then joined CINBIO, University of Vigo, as a postdoctoral researcher, where I developed drug delivery systems to overcome antimicrobial resistance. I am now a Xunta de Galicia postdoctoral fellow, carrying out my research at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, studying how to develop materials that control chemical reactions.
