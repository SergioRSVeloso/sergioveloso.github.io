About

I am a researcher trained in biochemistry, biophysics and bionanosystems, and physics. My work brings together soft matter and nanomaterials to design stimuli-responsive systems for drug delivery, theranostics and light-driven catalysis.

I obtained my PhD in Physics through the MAP-fis doctoral programme at the Centre of Physics of the University of Minho (Portugal). I have since worked as a postdoctoral researcher at CINBIO, University of Vigo (Spain), where I currently hold a Xunta de Galicia postdoctoral fellowship, and carried out a postdoctoral stay at the Institute of Chemistry of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem (Israel) in the group of Prof. Itamar Willner.

Languages: Portuguese · English · Spanish · Galician

Research

Soft matter for drug delivery & theranostics

Magnetic and plasmonic lipogels, self-assembled peptide hydrogels, polyelectrolyte gels and hybrid lipid–polymer nanoparticles that release drugs in response to pH, heat, alternating magnetic fields or near-infrared light.

Peptide hydrogelsLipogelsMagnetic NPsPlasmonic NPs3D spheroids

Photocatalytic microcapsules

Fe³⁺-crosslinked carboxymethyl-cellulose microcapsules, including molecularly imprinted and metal-loaded capsules, coupled to photosystems and flavins to drive light-powered redox reactions.

Hydrogel microcapsulesPhotosystem IFlavinsNitrate-to-ammonia

Modelling drug release

Reaction–diffusion models of release from slabs and spheres, solved by eigenfunction expansion, Laplace and asymptotic methods, linking material parameters to release kinetics and to empirical models (Weibull, Korsmeyer–Peppas, Higuchi).

Reaction–diffusionRelease kineticsMathematical modelling

Drug-eluting contact lenses & nanocarriers

Polymeric, lipid, protein and mesoporous-silica carriers, and their integration into ophthalmic devices for sustained local delivery.

PLGA–PEGChitosanBSA/HAContact lenses
