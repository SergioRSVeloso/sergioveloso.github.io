About

From biochemistry to physics, and back to the clinic

Trained in biochemistry, biophysics and bionanosystems, and physics, I design stimuli-responsive gels, nanoparticles and hybrid carriers that release drugs on demand, in response to pH, heat, magnetic fields or near-infrared light.

I hold a Xunta de Galicia postdoctoral fellowship at CINBIO, University of Vigo, after a PhD at the University of Minho and a postdoctoral stay with Prof. Itamar Willner at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.
